# REQ-001: Sort the product list

**As a** shopper
**I want** to sort the product list
**so that** I can find the cheapest product, or a product by name, quickly.

## Acceptance criteria

1. When the list opens, products are sorted by name, A to Z.
2. When I choose "Price (low to high)", the cheapest product is first and no
   product is cheaper than the one before it.
3. When I choose "Price (high to low)", the most expensive product is first
   and no product is more expensive than the one before it.
4. When I choose "Name (Z to A)", the names are in reverse alphabetical order.
5. Sorting never adds or removes a product.
